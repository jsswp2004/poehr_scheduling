# 🏥 Complete Guide: How to Register Your Organization with POWER Healthcare

*A step-by-step guide to getting your healthcare practice set up and running*

---

## 📋 What You'll Need Before You Start

Before beginning your registration, please have the following information ready:

### **Organization Information**
- ✅ Your practice or clinic's official name
- ✅ Business address (street, city, state, zip code)
- ✅ Main phone number
- ✅ Business email address
- ✅ Website URL (if you have one)

### **Administrator Details**
- ✅ Your full name (as the main administrator)
- ✅ Your professional email address
- ✅ Your direct phone number
- ✅ Your job title/role in the organization

### **Subscription Information**
- ✅ Credit card or payment method
- ✅ Billing address (if different from business address)

---

## 🚀 Step-by-Step Registration Process

### **Step 1: Start Your Registration**

1. **Visit the POWER Healthcare website**
   - Go to your organization's POWER platform URL
   - Click the **"Get Started"** or **"Register Organization"** button

2. **Choose Your Plan**
   - Review the available subscription plans
   - Select the plan that best fits your organization's size and needs
   - Click **"Choose This Plan"**

---

### **Step 2: Enter Your Organization Details**

You'll now fill out a form with your practice information:

#### **Basic Information**
- **Organization Name**: Enter your clinic or practice's official name
- **Address**: Provide your main business location
- **Phone Number**: Your main practice phone number
- **Email**: A general contact email for your organization
- **Website**: Your practice website (optional)

#### **Organization Type**
- Select what type of healthcare organization you are:
  - Private Practice
  - Clinic
  - Hospital Department
  - Medical Group
  - Other (specify)

---

### **Step 3: Set Up Your Administrator Account**

As the person registering, you'll become the main administrator:

#### **Personal Information**
- **Full Name**: Your complete name
- **Email Address**: Your professional email (this will be your login)
- **Phone Number**: Your direct contact number
- **Job Title**: Your role (e.g., Practice Manager, Administrator, Doctor)

#### **Create Your Password**
- Choose a strong password (at least 8 characters)
- Include uppercase and lowercase letters, numbers, and symbols
- Confirm your password

---

### **Step 4: Billing and Payment Setup**

#### **Choose Your Billing Cycle**
- **Monthly**: Pay each month (more flexibility)
- **Annual**: Pay yearly (usually includes a discount)

#### **Payment Information**
- **Credit Card Number**: Enter your payment card details
- **Expiration Date**: MM/YY format
- **Security Code**: The 3-4 digit code on your card
- **Cardholder Name**: Name as it appears on the card

#### **Billing Address**
- Enter the address associated with your payment method
- Check the box if it's the same as your organization address

---

### **Step 5: Review and Confirm**

#### **Double-Check Everything**
- Review all organization information
- Verify your contact details
- Confirm your subscription plan and billing information
- Read the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy

#### **Submit Your Registration**
- Check the box to agree to the terms
- Click **"Complete Registration"**

---

## ✅ What Happens Next?

### **Immediate Confirmation**
- You'll see a success message on screen
- A confirmation email will be sent to your administrator email
- Your account will be activated within a few minutes

### **Welcome Email**
You'll receive a welcome email containing:
- Your login credentials
- Link to access your POWER dashboard
- Getting started checklist
- Contact information for support

### **Account Setup**
Once logged in, you can:
- Add other staff members to your account
- Upload your provider schedules
- Set up your appointment types
- Configure your notification preferences

---

## 🔧 Getting Started After Registration

### **First Login**
1. Check your email for the welcome message
2. Click the login link or go to your organization's POWER URL
3. Enter your email and password
4. You'll be taken to your dashboard

### **Initial Setup Tasks**
1. **Add Staff Members**
   - Go to "Team Management"
   - Invite doctors, nurses, and administrative staff
   - Set their roles and permissions

2. **Upload Schedules**
   - Navigate to "Schedule Management"
   - Upload provider availability
   - Set up appointment types and durations

3. **Configure Settings**
   - Set your time zone
   - Choose notification preferences
   - Customize appointment reminders

4. **Test the System**
   - Create a test appointment
   - Send a test reminder
   - Familiarize yourself with the dashboard

## 🆘 Need Help?

### **Common Questions**

**Q: What if I make a mistake during registration?**
A: Don't worry! You can contact support to update any information after registration.

**Q: Can I change my subscription plan later?**
A: Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time from your account settings.

**Q: What if my payment doesn't go through?**
A: Check your card details and try again. If issues persist, contact your bank or our support team.

## 🎯 Tips for Success

### **Before You Register**
- Have all information gathered and ready
- Choose a quiet time when you won't be interrupted
- Use a secure internet connection
- Consider who else in your organization should have administrator access

### **After Registration**
- Save your login information in a secure place
- Schedule time to explore the platform
- Plan your staff training sessions
- Start with a small pilot group before full implementation

### **Best Practices**
- Keep your contact information updated
- Regularly review your subscription and usage
- Take advantage of training resources
- Provide feedback to help improve the platform
